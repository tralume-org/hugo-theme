This is the "About" page of the Tralume example site.

## About This Site

This site is built with the [Tralume](https://forgejo.alexma.top/tralume-org/hugo-theme) theme — a modern Hugo theme based on Material Design 3.

## About Tralume

The Tralume theme strives to provide:

- **Ready to use**: Clone and run, no extra build tools needed.
- **Full i18n**: Built-in en-US and zh-Hans translations.
- **Rich features**: Search, comments, friends links, analytics, AI disclosure, and more.
- **Visual style**: Acrylic/frosted glass surfaces with Material Design 3 tokens.

The footer automatically shows copyright info, an RSS subscribe link, and any custom entries you configure in `hugo.toml`.
